THROUGHLINE

A coach that plans the day and waits for your yes.

ThroughLine proposes a plan each morning from your goals and your calendar. Nothing counts until you approve it. Each evening it reviews what actually happened, and what slipped flows into the days ahead.

Screens captured from the live build on October 8, 2026. These are pictures, not a working demo. A few personal details are covered.

On the phone

Four tabs sit at the bottom of the screen: Today, Goals, Coach and Settings. The app can follow the device, stay in one mode, or switch by time of day.

How to read the screen

  • Goals across every horizonThe row of tiles holds one goal each for the week, month, quarter and year, with a word and a marker for where it stands.
  • One timeline for the dayWork toward a goal carries a blue bar on its left edge. Fixed commitments from the calendar sit beside it in plain cards.
  • Honest about what it cannot seeA calendar block with hidden details is drawn hatched and titled "Contents unknown". The plan never invents what is inside it.
  • A proposal until approvedThe banner changes only when the owner approves. After that the day is set, and the evening review compares it to what happened.
  • Four tabs, one job eachToday holds the plan. Goals holds the targets. Coach is the conversation. Settings holds the times and the appearance choice.
  • Goals change by conversationThe goal list is read only on purpose. Targets move through a coaching conversation, never quietly.

On a wider screen

The same four tabs move to the top bar, and the goals, body readings and to-dos sit beside the timeline.

Settings

  • Plan time and review timeWhen the morning plan arrives and when the evening review opens.
  • Day job hoursGoal work is planned in the open margins around the workday: before, at lunch, and after.
  • Two notifications a dayThe plan is ready in the morning, and a nudge to review at night.
  • AppearanceSystem, Light, Dark, or By time of day. The choice is saved on each device.
